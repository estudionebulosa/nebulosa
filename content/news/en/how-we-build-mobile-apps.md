At Nebulosa Estudio we believe a great app doesn't start with code — it starts with the problem. We've spent over 8 years designing and building mobile apps for iOS and Android, and during that time we've refined a process that focuses on what really matters: solving real problems and creating apps people want to use.

## 1. Discovery: understand before building

Every project starts with a discovery phase. We sit down with the client — or with our own team — and answer three fundamental questions: what problem are we solving, who are we solving it for, and how do we measure success? Without clear metrics from day one, it's impossible to know if the product works.

In this phase we deliver a product document with the vision, goals, target audience, and a first prioritized backlog. We also research the market: are there competing apps? What do they do well? What gap can we fill?

## 2. Design: prototype, test, iterate

We move on to designing the experience. We start with low-fidelity wireframes to validate the navigation flow, then move up to interactive prototypes in Figma. Before writing a single line of code, we test those prototypes with real users.

This stage saves us months of rework. It's much cheaper to change a prototype than to change production code. The result is a complete design system: colors, typography, components, and key screens ready for development.

## 3. Build: sprints, demos, transparency

We work in two-week sprints with continuous delivery. Each sprint ends with a live demo where we show what was built, collect feedback, and adjust as needed. We use Swift and Kotlin for native apps, and React Native or Flutter when time-to-market is the priority.

Our code follows strict standards: peer review, automated tests, continuous integration. We never ship anything that hasn't been tested.

## 4. Launch: more than just publishing

Publishing on Google Play and the App Store is just the beginning. Before launch we optimize the app listing (ASO) with keywords, screenshots, and descriptions designed to convert. We set up monitoring with tools like Sentry and Firebase Crashlytics to catch issues before users report them.

## 5. Growth: iterate with data

A living app is an app that keeps improving. We analyze usage metrics, user reviews, and retention data to prioritize the next releases. Every update targets a specific metric: 7-day retention, conversion rate, or session time.

> "80% of an app's success is decided before the first line of code is written."

That's our approach. If you have an app idea and want us to help bring it to life, we'd love to hear from you.
